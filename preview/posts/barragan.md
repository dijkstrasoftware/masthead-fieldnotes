Shot over three slow days with one camera and two rolls. Nothing staged, mostly waiting for the light to do something.

The house had been empty for a year, and it still smelled of the last summer someone lived in it. Curtains half-drawn, a lamp nobody had moved.

> The rooms were quiet in a way that made you whisper.

A few frames from the second roll, which came back warmer than expected.
